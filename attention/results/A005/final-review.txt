Main-result stage

The saved main archive passed 3,202,431 independent checks with no errors.
The auditor reconstructs action eligibility, exact C2 greedy choice, paid
replacement priority, charges and package budgets, irreversible closures,
P-strict evidence, independent truth labels, frozen S3 category forecasts,
monitor residuals, alert locations, onset-relative exposures and delays,
per-package outcomes, summary arithmetic, and Dprime equivalence.

The auditor was extended after main execution only to strengthen verification:
streaming archive input, C2 choice, replacement priority, original-observation
crosschecks, and replacement arithmetic. These changes do not modify the
experimental scheduler, thresholds, numerical executors, or saved outcomes.
The independently authored auditor already existed before main execution.

replay remains separate. Exhaustive B tree optimality is not independently
recomputed, and numerical solver agreement is not physical validation.
