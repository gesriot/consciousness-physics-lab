# B003: provenance-guided self-checks

## Registration and question

This prospective protocol precedes new B003 implementation and outcomes. Commit
it before implementation, and commit the development admission freeze before
confirmation intake generation. Preserve failures and disclose departures from
this chronology. The question is whether standing dependencies can direct paid
self-checks, followed by qualified revision, better than simpler schedules under
the same work allowance. No external failure notification is supplied.

B002 qualified clean graded finite volumes for failed collocation and admitted
whole-family permissive corruption at 4 packages. Reuse that instrument, domain,
guards and full fixed grid charges without tuning or requalification. Record hashes
of binding/results/B002/qualification-freeze.json and
binding/results/B002/exposure-first/freeze.json. Do not narrow the admitted domain.

## Populations, streams and admission

Use 12 development series, seeds 20270201 through 20270212, and 12 confirmation
series, seeds 20270213 through 20270224. A series is the independent unit. Packages,
conditions, schedules, quotas and durations within it are paired dependent
observations. Use the B002 five-cohort distributions, five requests per package and
nine candidate conditions. Reset the stale predecessor between series. Verify no
exact request overlap with B001/B002 development or confirmation and between B003
development and confirmation.

Spawn five separate streams for requests, condition order, sample execution seeds,
single-channel target and RR initial phase. Draw the target once per series uniformly from the six
collocation channels, independently of all other streams. Archive its provenance
for evaluation, never for scheduling. Draw the RR initial phase uniformly from all
CHANNELS indices using its separate stream, independently of the fault target and
requests. Share this phase across quotas and scenarios within each series and
archive the phase, seed and stream identity. Requests and sample-pool rules are shared
across paired policies. Longer durations preserve the exact request/condition/target
prefix. No schedule receives evaluator truth, target, onset or affected labels.

Environments are whole-family collocation permissiveness, single-channel
collocation permissiveness and clean. Package 0 is clean; corruption starts before
the first action of package 1 and persists. Use the B002 permissive verdict hook,
not a stronger or tuned fault. Whole-family duration 4 is inherited from B002.

For single-channel admission run development N0 ordinary work without alarms for
predefined durations 4, 8 and 16 under the work rule below. At each duration pool
unique accepted identities across the 12 series, not repeated policies or duration
copies. Require at least 10 acceptances actually supported by corrupted observations,
including at least 5 false acceptances. Select the shortest passing duration.
Retain all failed candidates. If none passes, exclude single-channel confirmation
and report its prediction untested; do not strengthen the fault. Commit complete
gates, chosen duration and hashes before generating confirmation intakes. Clean
controls use each distinct admitted duration. Empty admission is not success.

## Shared budget and full actual cost

For each package compute the B002 clean full-pipeline native work cost on its fixed
inputs independently of schedule and fault. This common package allowance applies
to every schedule and quota, including N0. There is no supplementary recall or
post-alarm purse. Ordinary checks, samples, clean replacements, retrospective review
and future suspect-dependent checks all draw from this allowance. Unused package
allowance does not carry forward; pending review eligibility and its series cap do.

Active quotas are 1, 2 and 4 samples per package; N0 has zero. Reference its same
run across quota cells without multiplying independent observations. Initially
reserve quota times the maximum native sample price; release unused reservation
after each sample to ordinary work. Never overdraw unreserved allowance. If an
intake cannot fund its quota, report a design failure rather than reducing quota
or dropping the series.

Charge samples their executed native work, including internal steps. Qualified
collocation replacements pay all grids 64, 128, 256, 512, 1024, 2048 and 4096 even
if decisive earlier or cached. Other replacements pay their frozen native prices.
Cache hits never reduce logical charges. Reference construction is evaluator
overhead, reported separately from operational work and elapsed runtime.

Archive mutually exclusive cost categories: samples, ordinary native checks,
ordinary clean replacements, retrospective recall, and future suspect-dependent
checks. Their sum is actual operational cost; reconcile it with the action ledger,
allowance and unused remainder. No replacement is counted twice inside ordinary
cost. Report actual-cost differences and outcome/cost dominance in each cell.
Equal sample counts are not equal price. Different discrete actions may leave
different remainders: report equal-budget evidence honestly. Do not pad idle work
to manufacture equal spending. Claim an outcome advantage at no higher price only
when S actual cost is no higher than RR; if S costs more, describe the tradeoff
without an equal-price advantage claim. Always give actual-cost differences.

## Common scheduling and temporal state

Use the same deterministic ordinary selector and sampling opportunities for every
active schedule. Sample k is due after paid ordinary work reaches k / quota times
the initial non-sample allowance, for k = 0 through quota minus 1, or when no
ordinary action is affordable. There are no policy-specific anticipated opportunities.
Process closures before actions at the same position. Include already closed
accepted inputs of this package and standing acceptances from earlier packages.
Use global half-tick timestamps as in B001/B002.

S counts distinct standing accepted records with raw executable-channel support c
newer than the last successful sample of c, divided by the next executable sample's
maximum native price. Count a record once per channel, not once per numerical
step. Do not multiply stakes by replacement price, age, latent accuracy or hidden
fault information. Successful markers persist across packages and advance only on
agreement with the sample standard. A mismatch never resets them. Independently
replaced support is not a raw dependency on the suspect channel.

Round every scheduling score to 9 decimals before deterministic ties. S chooses
highest score, then oldest last successful sample, then CHANNELS index. Initial
markers are -1, so successful zero-stake choices rotate instead of repeating one
channel indefinitely. A mismatching channel becomes suspect. Ordinary/replacement
price-ranking keys also use round(price, 9), then input index and fixed action or
channel order. Charge original native prices, not rounded ranking keys. Do not
change frozen scientific numerical decision rules.

Use frozen A007/B002 executable sample pools and boundary/nonaccept/accept cycling.
Pool cursors persist across packages. Know the next recipe before using its price;
never choose it from hidden fault information. Archive stake numerators, price
denominators, rounded scores and tie keys at every opportunity.

RR traverses the fixed CHANNELS cycle from its independently drawn initial phase,
with a pointer persistent across packages. This retains the A007 fixed-order,
random-phase design rather than restarting the same prefix each package. Detection
can still fail or be censored within the admitted duration; do not force a visit to
the target or extend a series to rescue the prediction.
BU retains the B002 score_channels formula, B002 cross prices, score rounding and
tie rule. It scores open inputs using replacement-price exposure and ordinary-action
age, not graph stakes. Its age counters reset each package exactly as in B002.
Common sample opportunities and persistent safety state are explicit B003 changes;
these are not byte-identical B002 trajectories. N0 takes no samples. Active policies
skip suspect channels; RR advances past them. Exhaustion of every channel is an
unsupported-state failure, not a new fallback. Ordinary selection never uses truth.

## Typed provenance and paired interventions

All schedules, including N0, retain the same typed provenance schema. Existing
executed channel-support edges are derived exactly as in B001/B002. Additionally
make the existing conclusion-to-original-request relation explicit as one
request-origin edge per accepted record. Its endpoint is the canonical original
request identity and its request hash; its timestamp is the closure timestamp.
The edge does not represent a newly executed check or independent physical evidence.
Request resolution at closure and review uses this edge and validates its endpoint
and hash against the canonical intake. Missing, ambiguous or changed request
identity is an audit failure. The added type is operational provenance, not an
unused display copy. It does not alter numeric-channel graph derivation.

Executable-channel support alone is eligible for self-check stakes: a request-origin
edge has no executable calibration channel or sample price. Both types retain a
raw/independent provenance label. On request-origin edges this label records only
the asserted provenance relation; toggling it cannot change the canonical endpoint,
request hash, numerical candidate, or request-resolution validity and does not
certify independent physical evidence. The same type filter is used by all policies.

At each sample opportunity identify standing records with raw collocation support
newer than that channel's last successful sample. In Sx toggle the relevant support
label to independent. In Sxprime toggle the request-origin raw/independent label
of precisely the same records. Each eligible record must have exactly one such
collocation support edge and one request-origin edge; ambiguity is a failure, not
permission to choose another control. There is one binary label edit per record in
both interventions, preserving each edge's endpoints, request hash and timestamp,
the graph size and the edited record identities. Edited edges have their own real
timestamps; do not claim their timestamps are identical across edge types.

Interventions use the preregistered collocation family in every environment, never
the hidden single target. They test collocation stake, not arbitrary graph damage.
They are recomputed from the original current graph, not accumulated. Archive
both edited graphs, edit lists and all-channel scores. S continues to score all
executable channels. Sxprime must leave every score and ranking key unchanged on
the same snapshot; Sx removes every eligible collocation match, not a partial subset.
Only scheduling sees the edits. G recall and request execution use authentic
unmodified provenance for every schedule, isolating the scheduling intervention.

This is a new typed B003 negative control, not the B002 normalization-edge control.
Normalization is executable support and could change global S stakes; it is not
irrelevant here. Sxprime's null score effect is guaranteed by the registered type
rule and is a specificity check, not independent evidence for a benefit of S.
No claims of preserving provenance-label frequency distributions are made.

## Alarms, review and future acceptances

A sample disagreement marks only that channel suspect. No family expansion is
used, even for whole-family corruption; the rule is identical in clean conditions
and across schedules. Suspect state persists. G eligibility uses supporting time
newer than the marker before the alarm, never hidden onset. Queue standing records
by newest supporting observation, newest record and identity, as in B002, with
rounded scores. Simultaneous notices use CHANNELS order. Deduplicate identities
across notices and never charge two retrospective attempts for one accepted identity.

The fixed recall cap is 32 qualified attempts per series across all alarms, subject
also to the common monetary allowance. It is not per channel or package. Pending
G review has priority after due samples; an unaffordable review may wait while
an affordable ordinary action proceeds. Recheck standing eligibility when dequeued.
Archive pending queues, attempts and both remaining budgets. Qualified review uses
the original request and candidate, not a corrupted observation. Protected acceptance
retains the identity with independent support; violation or abstention withdraws it.
Keep the immutable historical record and revision event. S sees updated standing
support. Lack of funds never silently withdraws an unreviewed old acceptance.

New acceptances supported by a suspect channel require paid qualified cross-checks
for every such channel. Protected acceptance closes with independent support;
violation rejects; abstention or insufficient money leaves the input unresolved.
These future-work checks do not consume the 32 retrospective attempts, but pay
full price from the same allowance. Never implement the A007 blanket channel block.
Non-numerical false alarms use existing independent clean re-execution at full
native price. Numerical alarms use the B002 qualified cross instrument.

## Measures and preregistered predictions

Primary series/cell measures are false acceptances remaining at series end,
accurate accepted identities subsequently withdrawn, and full actual operational
cost. Report accurate ordinary acceptances never obtained separately from losses
of previously accepted identities. Also report initial acceptances and correct
final acceptances; abstention is not retention.

Secondary measures are detection delay in packages, post-onset fraction of samples
on corrupted channels, clean false alarms and their cost, and correct ordinary
results lost through sampling expenditure. Detection in package 1 has delay 0;
undetected corruption receives penalty D, the series duration. Report censoring
separately; a penalty is not observed detection time. Sample fractions use all
post-onset samples; N0's denominator is zero and its fraction undefined.

Opportunity cost uses correct final identity-set differences from paired clean N0
at the same allowance and duration. Report gross lost, gross gained and net counts.
Also report faulty-environment differences without attributing them solely to samples,
since alarms and rechecks also change work. False-alarm cost includes mismatch
samples and directly triggered reviews/rechecks. Report all clean sample expenditure
separately. Preserve all per-series values, totals and paired schedule-minus-S
differences with descriptive standard errors over 12 series. Describe single-target
strata without assuming balanced random draws. Dependent variants are not replicates.

Predictions fixed before outcomes:

1. S detects whole-family corruption in its first faulty package in a majority of
   series at every quota 1, 2 and 4.
2. BU repeats its B002 failure to detect collocation corruption.
3. RR detects whole-family corruption, but at quota 1 later than S.
4. S leaves fewer false acceptances than RR for single-channel corruption at
   quotas 1 and 2.
5. Sx loses the advantage; Sxprime preserves it.
6. There are no false alarms in the clean environment.

## Closure and interpretation

For admitted corruption by quota cells, compare total remaining false acceptances
and lost accurate accepted identities. If RR is no worse than S on both in every
primary cell, state directly that graph stakes are unnecessary for allocating
self-checks in this laboratory. Apply this criterion independently of actual price,
which remains a third primary measure. Do not substitute favorable detection or
other secondary measures. Report cost limitations on equal-price interpretation.
Excluded single-channel corruption remains untested. Clean-only or empty admission
cannot satisfy closure vacuously. Distinguish observation, computation and interpretation.

The interpretation joins attention as selection of checks to binding as selection
of conclusions to revisit: a functional dependency-map role. It is not Graziano's
attention schema, a cortical model or a theory of consciousness.
