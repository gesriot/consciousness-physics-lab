# B002: qualification before new recall data

## Registration and stopping rule

This protocol is committed before implementation or inspection of new qualification
outputs. Only the twelve frozen B001 development series are used for instrument
qualification. Confirmation records are not used. These are development data,
not new independent evidence for a binding effect. Parameters below cannot be
changed after results are inspected. A failed instrument is excluded explicitly.
If neither instrument qualifies, stop before exposure generation and confirmation.
If no qualified instrument has a passing exposure candidate, stop before confirmation.
Negative findings and intermediate records are retained.

## Independent replacement candidates

For a failed collocation channel use clean graded finite volumes with the frozen
C1 convergence rule and grids 64, 128, 256, 512, 1024, 2048, 4096. For a failed V2
channel use clean radial collocation at degrees 64 and 128. Both solve the sphere
model from the original request, not the corrupted observation or model. Independence
means independence from that injected channel fault, not a new physical model.
No uniform-grid replacement is used by either candidate.

The V2 native uncertainty is twice its last graded-grid difference plus its own
rounding allowance R = 32 epsilon n^2 max(1, |response|). Starting at n = 512,
require C1 and both a decision margin and contraction margin greater than 4 R.
The decision margin is tolerance minus discrepancy minus uncertainty for acceptance,
or discrepancy minus uncertainty minus tolerance for violation. The contraction
margin is half the preceding difference minus the last difference. Return the first
protected decisive result; otherwise evaluate through 4096 and abstain. The test
uses the graded-grid differences themselves, not the uniform-grid differences or
its terminal cell count. Its contraction must be resolved above its native floor.

Collocation uses its own native allowance R = 32 epsilon 128^4 max(1, |response|),
uncertainty twice the degree difference plus R, and convergence degree difference
less than or equal to tolerance / 8. Its protected convergence margin is tolerance / 8
minus the degree difference. Require both this and the decision margin greater than
4 R. There is no fabricated third degree or finite-volume contraction test.
All quantities must be finite. The operational domain of both instruments is the
whole original finite right half-plane sphere request domain; out-of-domain or
nonfinite inputs abstain and do not silently disappear from qualification counts.

## Qualification data and gates

Form the union of accepted candidate identities across the three archived B001
development scenarios. An identity is (series, package, input index); repeated
policies, budgets and faults do not multiply observations. Reconstruct the original
request and candidate from each archived package. Physical accuracy is determined
only by the archived independent reference audit, never by replacement output.
Evaluate both replacements for every identity. The primary denominator is every
accurate accepted identity in this union. A candidate qualifies only if:

1. At least 80% of those identities receive a protected decisive verdict, with at
   least one protected acceptance, so an always-abstaining instrument cannot pass.
2. No accurate identity is declared a violation and no inaccurate identity is accepted.
3. There are zero dangerous coverage misses on its corresponding existing complete
   map: E014 first map for graded V2 and E015 map for collocation. Replay each stored
   numerical path, not just the old summary. For every mapped point and every native
   V2 step or collocation tolerance plane where the protected convergence gate passes,
   require actual reference error no greater than the instrument uncertainty.
4. A sequential last-bit-noise replay preserves all protected verdicts and gate
   outcomes. Perturb real and imaginary solver outputs by one representable step
   before derived arithmetic. Numerical diagnostics may differ and are summarized.

Maps are coverage challenges across their original sampled domains, not proof of
continuous coverage. Their source hashes, counts and uncovered examples are retained.
No boundary or failed region is removed after inspection. A qualification result
must list exact denominators and each failure rather than report only an overall flag.

## Exposure candidates, fixed before generation

Only qualified fault families proceed to a new development exposure check. Use
12 independent series with seeds 20270101 through 20270112, generated by the same
five-cohort request distributions and nine candidate conditions as B001. Three
predefined durations are 4, 8 and 16 packages of five requests. Package zero is
clean and every remaining package is faulty. One candidate corrupts the entire
collocation family to always accept; the other corrupts the entire V2 family by
replacing uncertainty by one hundredth. No additional severity is tried. Ordinary
work uses the frozen BU policy, quota 10 and 100% clean package budget, with the
same clean cross replacement after an alarm as for retrospective review.

Each family/duration is evaluated over all 12 development series. An exposure
passes only with at least 10 accepted identities genuinely supported by corrupted
observations, including at least 5 inaccurate accepted identities. Count identities,
not repeated policies. For each qualified family select the shortest passing duration;
ties use the listed family order. Retain all failures. No passing candidate means no
confirmation. A family without both gates is excluded, not silently treated as tested.
Commit the selected instruments, durations, complete exposure results and their
hashes before generating confirmation intakes.

## Reproducibility

Use only binding/ for new implementation; lab/ and attention/ remain instruments.
The first implementation includes --last-bit-noise and a nonempty --runtime-override.
Normal and perturbed runs are sequential. Save every completed series or map batch
before continuing. Scores are rounded to nine decimals before deterministic ties.
Archive finite diagnostics separately from exact verdicts, flags, counts and prices.
