# B001: instrument recall and provenance

## Registration and scope

Registered before implementation and new development or confirmation data.
The hypothesis concerns functional provenance, not neural synchronization or a
theory of consciousness. Existing E/A controls, physical reference audits,
immutable attention instruments, independent guarded replacement, negative results,
rounded scores and deterministic ties remain mandatory.

## Units and fixed environment

One independent series contains four packages of five requests, one from each
A007 cohort, with all nine existing artifact conditions. The stale predecessor
starts afresh in each series. Packages, conditions, memories and budgets within a
series are paired and dependent. Request distributions are exactly A007's.
Development seeds are 20261206–20261217; confirmation seeds 20261218–20261229.
Each seed independently generates requests, condition order and sample randomization.
Each package has 100% of its full clean C1 cost and the unchanged A007 BU
active-sample quota and scheduler. Every memory gets the identical ordinary trace,
paid sample schedule, future-channel protection and package budget. BU scheduling
resets inside each package as in A007; the retrospective ledger, last successful
sample times and previous alarm history persist across packages. This deliberate
separation tests retrospective binding, not improved prospective detection.

Package 0 is clean. Faults start at the first ordinary action of package 1 and
persist through package 3. Agents never receive that onset. Scenarios are no fault
with no external notice, no fault with a false external notice, always-accepting
local collocation, and local V2 U/100. Primary notification occurs after package 3
and names the faulty channel only. Secondary recall uses the first actual BU alarm
for each channel, at its actual action time, with no external notification.
Successful calibration means observed verdict equals frozen expected verdict,
not merely a calibration attempt. The global clock is the ordered action index
across packages; samples and replacements occupy time. Unsampled channels have
last success -1. Log both channel-specific and global minimum last-good times.

## Development and freeze

Development has two independent series per candidate band, seed index modulo six
selecting its local band, for both fault families. After all development runs,
select one band per family by greatest number of false acceptances with actual
post-onset supporting observations in that channel; ties use greater affected
acceptance count, then lower band index. Preserve all candidates and counts.
The exposure target is at least 10 affected acceptances and 5 affected false
acceptances in the selected candidate's two series. If not met, still freeze and
run confirmation without changing distributions, budget, severity or onset;
report the failure and interpret results only at the observed exposure level.
The freeze artifact is committed before confirmation. No tuning after confirmation.

## Records and interventions

An acceptance stores its identity, closure clock, each actually executed accepting
support channel and region, observation clocks and last successful sample clock
at acceptance. Terminal V2 supports the V2 channel; completed accepting feature,
binding, modal and collocation observations support their respective channels.
Rejected/inconclusive candidates never enter memory. Separate evaluator records
hold physical accuracy and which supporting observations actually encountered the
fault. Neither evaluator truth nor fault onset is passed to recall selection.

G selects records having a supporting observation on the notified channel after
its latest successful sample before notification. Within this dependency set use
most recent supporting observation, then most recent acceptance, then stable ID.
T stores the same acceptance records but no channel links and selects every record
closed after that channel's latest successful sample, newest first. A selects every
past acceptance newest first. N selects none. A is an exhaustive upper-bound only
when its finite budget suffices; it is not assumed safer at limited budgets.

For each notice Gx relabels the notified-channel edge of every eligible record to
the next channel in its same six-band family. Gxprime changes one unrelated feature
edge on exactly those same records to another feature channel. Exactly one edge
changes per selected record in both interventions; the integer channel-index L1
change is one in both (use predecessor at the endpoint). Timestamps are unchanged.
The intervention uses recorded links and the notified channel, never accuracy,
onset or affected labels. Both corrupted records and intervention audits are saved.

## Replacement and budgets

Recall re-executes A007 guarded_uniform on the frozen candidate through an
independent uniform-grid instrument (4096, 8192, 16384, 32768). Accept retains the
claim; violation or inconclusive withdraws it. Every attempted replacement is
charged its full A007 replacement cost even if a numerical result is cached.
Budgets are 2, 8 and 32 such replacements per series, shared in amount across all
memories, separate from ordinary package budgets. Secondary multiple alarms consume
one cumulative budget; previously reviewed records are not reviewed again.
Selection and execution happen sequentially at the notification clock; delay is
reported in replacement service slots, not invented wall-clock time. Scores round
to nine decimal places before deterministic ties; last-bit perturbations are
available from the first implementation. Unused budget is not reallocated.

## Outcomes and decision

Primary by scenario and budget: false accepts still standing, accurate accepts
withdrawn, and paid recall cost. Secondary: precision and recall of actual
withdrawals against the true set of accepted claims supported by a fault-exposed
observation before notification; separate selection coverage; service delay and
censoring; no-notice behavior must show zero recalls. False acceptances unrelated
to the faulty supporting channel remain in the primary residual metric.

Report per-series counts, paired differences and their mean/standard error across
12 independent series. Conditions never become independent rows. Zero denominators
are null, not zero. No significance fishing or post hoc stopping.

Predictions: G approaches adequately funded A's safety at lower cost; T is
intermediate; N retains false accepts; Gx loses G's advantage and Gxprime does not.
Closure rule: compare T with G on both residual false accepts and unnecessary
correct withdrawals at every fixed primary faulty-scenario budget. If T is no
worse on both aggregated endpoints at all six cells, close with explicit statement
that links were unnecessary here. Otherwise report the exact cells supporting a
functional advantage, conditional on exposure; do not claim universal necessity.

## Audit and replay

Commit protocol before code, code before development, and freeze before confirmation.
Stream one package and series checkpoint at a time using deterministic gzip with
mtime zero; never reload all historical archives. Exact-input replay, a separate
last-bit-noise replay and --runtime-override are mandatory. Decisions are compared
exactly; floating diagnostics separately. Tests cover causality, corruption parity,
no-op, budgets, strict replacement, successful samples, time boundaries and replay.

Recall never feeds back to the shared upstream trace, including in the secondary
branch. The replacement is independent of the injected channel and uses uniform
rather than geometric grids, but shares A007's v2_step decision mathematics; no
claim of independence from all numerical failure mechanisms is made. Apparent
successful samples after a hidden onset remain successful in the ledger, without
oracle filtering, and can legitimately hide earlier affected claims.

The no-fault/no-notice and false-notice primary scenarios share one bit-identical
clean trace. The false notice names the selected collocation channel (development
uses its candidate collocation band). Zero-recall requirement applies to primary
no-notice only; secondary false BU alarms are retained. Relabeled links retain
unique edge IDs, including when the new channel duplicates another edge: this is
an edge-labeled multigraph with unchanged degree, not a deduplicated set. The
band-5 relevant edit maps to band 4. Ranking perturbations apply to recall as well
as the upstream scheduler. Selection uses strict observation-time > last success.

A successful clean required replacement supersedes the original dependency on
that channel. The accepted record instead stores an independent replacement edge
with its own namespace and timestamp; the replaced original observation cannot
make that acceptance fault-affected. This applies before any recall policy runs.
Failure of the closure criterion is not itself G dominance: conflicting endpoint
directions are a trade-off. Dominance needs both endpoints no worse and one better.

Secondary recall is scoped to BU alarms in V2 or collocation families. Other alarms
remain in the upstream archive but are not interpreted as numerical instrument
recalls: guarded uniform replacement does not certify provenance or model semantics.
Use NumPy SeedSequence(seed).spawn(3) for independent request, order and sample RNG
streams. Every series stores exact requests and sample descriptors. Confirmation
metadata includes the SHA256 of the committed development freeze artifact.
