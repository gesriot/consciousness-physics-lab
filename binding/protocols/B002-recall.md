# B002: revision with a qualified independent replacement

## Admission and frozen choices

This prospective comparison is registered before qualification or new B002 outcomes.
The qualification protocol is binding: only a fault family whose clean cross
instrument and exposure both passed may enter confirmation. Its shortest passing
predefined duration and instrument are frozen and committed first. Neither failure
licenses a new candidate, domain restriction or parameter search in this experiment.
If all families fail, B002 ends as a negative qualification result without a recall
comparison, and its predictions and closure criterion remain untested.

## Design

Use 12 new independent confirmation series with seeds 20270113 through 20270124.
The unit is a series; packages, candidate variants, memories, notification branches,
budgets and fault conditions within it are paired dependent observations. Sample
requests, condition order and BU sample randomization from separate seeded streams.
The stale predecessor is reset between series. Confirm no exact request overlap
with development. Ordinary policy and its paid clean replacement are shared across
memories. A retrospective decision cannot change future ordinary actions.

Compare G (provenance), T (time journal), A (all past acceptances), N (no review),
Gx (relevant links corrupted) and Gxprime (matched irrelevant links corrupted),
using the B001 deterministic ordering and matched edge interventions. For a whole
family fault an external notice names its six channels without revealing onset.
Order notices by channel index and use one shared budget of 2, 8 or 32 replacement
attempts per series. The other branch uses actual first BU alarms, without an
external notice. Repeated notices cannot charge the same accepted identity twice.
Include a clean no-notice environment and a clean false-notice environment.

Each selected acceptance is re-executed by the qualified cross instrument for the
notified family. Protected acceptance retains it; violation or abstention withdraws
it. Charge every attempt using the frozen action-model native work prices even if
results are cached. Fixed replacement work is charged through all declared grids
or both collocation degrees so stopping/caching cannot create a cost advantage.
Retain both operational verdicts and all diagnostic margins. Physical truth and
hidden fault onset are never passed to selection or to the replacement instrument.

## Measures and predictions

Primary measures are false acceptances still standing at series end, accurate
acceptances unnecessarily withdrawn, and paid replacement cost. Secondary measures
are protected confirmation fraction, precision and recall of selected identities
against the true fault-affected accepted set, and the number of unnecessary losses
removed relative to an otherwise identical B001 uniform replacement replay.
Report the original B001 historical numbers separately because populations and
fault exposure differ; do not attribute that historical difference solely to the
replacement. Report sums and per-series paired differences with descriptive
standard errors; dependent variants are not independent observations.

Predictions registered before data: a capable replacement sharply reduces
unnecessary losses across all reviewing memories. G retains a cost and residual
error advantage at small budgets. At large budgets T and A approach G in safety
but require more work. Relevant corruption Gx removes the selection advantage;
matched irrelevant corruption Gxprime preserves it. N is a no-review control,
not expected to lose correct acceptances.

## Closure and interpretation

For each admitted fault family and each matched budget in the external-notice
branch, compare T against G on total remaining false acceptances and total lost
accurate acceptances. If T is no worse on both measures in every primary cell,
explicit links are unnecessary in this laboratory, and the report must say so.
Empty admission does not satisfy this rule vacuously: it means the comparison was
not run and necessity was not tested. BU and false-notice results remain required
secondary checks, not post hoc replacements for this closure rule.

The interpretation concerns the functional role of linking a conclusion to its
support when revising it after instrument failure. It concerns provenance, not
neural synchronization or a theory of consciousness. Preserve negative results,
protocol chronology, all gate failures, and the distinction between development
selection and independent confirmation. Finish with sequential ordinary/noise
replays, explicit runtime override, independent archive auditing and the full checks.
