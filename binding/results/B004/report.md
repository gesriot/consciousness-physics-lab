# B004: source reconciliation

## Result and scope

Transitive provenance prioritizes the affected descendants better than the registered flat and temporal rules at limited budgets. The effect is selective revision in this laboratory, not consciousness, cortical synchronization, or necessity of every explicit graph representation.

All three fault types give identical action/count/cost summaries here: their magnitudes differ, but the same paired support chains cross the accuracy threshold. The tables below apply separately to units, regime and version; they are not three independent replications. There are 12 independent confirmation series, 48 original claims each, and 72 initially inaccurate claims per corruption. Budgets are 2/8/32 units of 69 ticks. Actual costs below are paid evaluation/bookkeeping ticks, not elapsed time.

## Depth and forecasts

At budget 2, G and G1 leave only the 24 depth-3 errors; F leaves 0/23/23 by depth. At budget 8, G leaves 0/0/0, G1 0/0/18, F 0/19/16. This is the predicted distinction between transitive, one-hop and flat provenance. At budget 32 all active rules exhaust the 48 records and recover every error; N keeps all 72 with no correct loss.

F and G both leave zero depth-1 errors at every budget. This supports the depth-1 accuracy forecast only; F can still inspect more correct roots. Gx loses the effect and matches F, while Gxprime matches G exactly as the registered structural placebo predicts.

G uses fewer aggregate paid ticks than F and T in the non-saturated cells, but costs are not exactly equal and per-series ordering is not uniformly favorable. The precise equal-price forecast was therefore not directly tested; common limits must not be described as identical expenditure. No mechanism or action was retuned after confirmation. At budget 8 in the conflict branch G loses 12 correct claims at end against F 11 and T 8: fewer errors is not domination on all safety measures.

The registered all-cell closure rule is false in 12 of 18 primary cells: F leaves more errors or makes more unnecessary withdrawals. The other six cells are budget-32 saturation. This conjunction failing is not by itself evidence that every forecast holds. The present positive finding comes from held-out deep-chain effects and the targeted graph intervention, subject to the assumptions below.

## Conflict localization and false notices

In each fault condition, all active policies locate 24 of 24 damaged supports in the conflict branch with 24 paid root evaluations and no incorrect support IDs. Units/version identify property tables; regime identifies calculation-method records, not innocent material tables. The comparison first establishes a numerical discrepancy and then checks typed metadata against a current catalogue. N does not diagnose. A pair is resolved when its remaining accepted endpoints no longer disagree; gold accuracy is measured separately.

All clean conflict runs have zero alarms and spend zero. False external notices cause unnecessary revisions. Correct original claims still withdrawn in G at budgets 2/8/32 are 12/11/0; F has 12/12/0 and T 10/12/0. At the largest budget all active memories redundantly rederive all 576 correct claims across the twelve series, leaving none withdrawn. The deliberate blind scan after priority candidates and the last unaffordable withdrawal are substantial costs of this registered policy, not a discovery that all revision systems need them.

Candidate precision/recall is recoverable from selected_bad/selected/initial_bad in each depth cell of summary.json. All integer counts and paired means/standard errors are preserved. [Paired effects](paired-effects.json) use series-level differences, not claims as independent samples.

## Design limits

This is a retrospective laboratory of already accepted finite-positive records, not an evaluation of initial source validation. Both source catalogues are accurate at recheck time; an incorrect shared definition or corrupted authoritative catalogue is outside the test. All corruptions exceed the registered tolerance, the independent comparison root is available, and derived claims are only three levels deep. The flat control is the explicit registered attribute classifier plus blind scan, not the class of all algorithms that could reconstruct lineage from numerical content. Equal memory means equal 48-record retention and a 2048-byte logical slot limit, not measured identical Python heap size.

The service takes an opaque claim ID and performs the full canonical recipe without exposing parent links to F/T. Immutable numerical results are memoized across paired conditions, but every policy pays the complete 1/67/68 evaluation price plus withdrawal and diagnosis. Physical cache-miss evaluations and complete action ledgers are retained. Common construction of initial records and hidden evaluation are outside the revision budget.
