# B005: localizing a faulty premise

## Result

Inherited provenance helps this laboratory diagnose and revise, but explicit parent links are not necessary when equivalent inherited labels fit in memory. G and L have identical selected operations, protected action sequences and primary outcomes in all 864 paired confirmation conditions. This is a negative result for necessity of the explicit graph representation, not for provenance itself.

Closure 1 is met: L is no worse than G on all four primary measures in every primary cell. Closure 2 is not met: F is worse than G/L in at least one primary measure in 26 of 36 cells (37 cell-measure failures). This does not establish uniform superiority of G/L. At budget 2, G leaves more errors than F for regime and intermediate faults; its total cost also exceeds F in some cells. The forecasts of universal lower price and of F being no worse at root signals are not confirmed.

## Design and units of evidence

There are 12 independent confirmation series, seeds 20270413–20270424, with 44 accepted claims each. Each series contains six fault conditions, shared/private variants, nine memory policies, three budgets and two signal branches: 7776 dependent conditions altogether. Shared/private outcomes are summed within a series before primary means and standard errors. Twelve development series are not additional confirmation observations. The complete [descriptive analysis](analysis.json) includes all policy cells, scope cells, per-depth localization, series-level standard errors, action equivalence and storage sizes.

A signal reports discrepant claims, never the culprit. Measurement selects failed independent observations; conflict selects discrepant alternative routes. For available signal depths sorted in increasing order, seed modulo their number selects one depth, retaining all signals at that depth. Root signals occur for units, version and regime faults, with three confirmation series per fault; intermediate and false-measurement signals start at depth 2. Different depths are not independent experiments.

G stores direct links; L stores inherited source and local-method labels. Both use the same set operations. F has direct attributes only; T uses reverse ingestion order; N does nothing. Gx/Lx replace relevant endpoint provenance, while Gxprime/Lxprime edit irrelevant metadata. Common numerical execution is outside the policy memory and exposes no hidden truth or graph to F/T.

Source checks cost 30 ticks for radius/material and 100 for method records. A local check pays for the specified operation and local-method access, using currently stored parents; it does not exonerate those parents. These are laboratory tariffs, not wall-clock measurements. Each available check tests one candidate, so the advertised partition score reduces here to a cost-based singleton test priority, with the registered tie rules. It is not a demonstration of powerful group testing. Budget limits are 2, 8 or 32 times the fixed deepest-claim unit. Actual expenditure differs. Rebuilding descendants pays the complete numerical operations; cheap diagnosis does not imply cheap complete revision.

## Localize first, then repair

At budget 8 with independent observations, G/L leave 0/0/7/0 errors for units/version/regime/intermediate; F leaves 130/180/129/91. With route conflicts G/L leave 4/6/80/31, versus F 130/185/129/91. At budget 32 G/L remove every physical error, while F still leaves 108/144/108/72. That last difference is not entirely a localization failure: F can identify a source but cannot select indirect descendants whose provenance it does not store. The localization columns below separate finding the culprit from repairing all inherited effects.

The lower-budget counterexamples matter: in measurement at budget 2, G leaves 180 regime errors versus F 160, and 108 intermediate errors versus F 104. At budget 32, regime measurement costs G 14120 ticks versus F 12560, while G repairs all errors and F leaves 108. Thus the result is not simultaneous dominance in price and accuracy. A failure of the all-cell closure condition is not proof of every positive forecast.

## False observations, clean controls and stopping

Clean conditions generate no signal, spend zero and retain all correct claims. The false-measurement condition leaves model claims correct. In its measurement branch, G/L resolve 0/24, 24/24 and 24/24 signals at budgets 2/8/32, costing 3528/12536/12536 ticks. F resolves 0/0/24, costing 3924/15730/46680; T resolves 0/0/24, costing 3886/15384/46680. Gx/Lx have no usable candidates and immediately repeat the observation: they resolve 24/24 at every budget for 3936 ticks. The corrupted representation is therefore cheaper on this special condition, but it leaves every physical fault unrepaired. N spends nothing and resolves none of the false measurement signals.

In the conflict branch a false observation does not change either calculated route, so there is no signal and no diagnostic action. Its zero errors and resolved flags are not evidence that the policy recognized an unreliable observation.

Final correct withdrawals are zero because repair is paid and completed before replacement; unaffordable rederivations do not destroy existing records. False accusations are zero for every policy, under the laboratory's correct catalogue and reliable local-step checks. Neither common zero supports a differential advantage of G. Suspected records remain accepted and are counted separately. Resolution stops checking; unlike B004 there is no deliberate blind continuation after resolution. Unused budget and total cost therefore carry information rather than being fixed by budget saturation.

## Representation, interventions and dependent patterns

G/L differ in zero of 864 selected and protected action sequences, and in zero primary outcomes. G/Gxprime and L/Lxprime each differ in zero of 864 protected action sequences. Relevant edits G/Gx and L/Lx each change 648 of 864 sequences. These interventions support a causal role for the available provenance, not necessity of its graph syntax.

G records occupy 156–174 bytes each, mean 168.5; L records 142–240, mean 181.5. Total serialized memory is 18545–18575 bytes for G and 19117–19147 for L; the matched logical capacity is 732027–732057 bytes including common source records. G saves 572 serialized bytes relative to L in matched unmodified views. Both fit without eviction. This is not equal resident memory, a speed benchmark or evidence that labels scale indefinitely.

Action equivalence is measured from selected operation kinds and target IDs, excluding numeric answers and scores. Of 648 fault-pair/policy/scope/budget/signal strata, 240 have identical sequences across all 12 paired series. N's empty action sequence and provenance-damaged policies' repeated action patterns are examples, not independent replications. Some intact-policy pairs also coincide for a subset of seeds; analysis.json lists every partition. Fault magnitude, altered source kind and remaining error count can differ despite an identical selected sequence.

## Limits and closure

Closure 1 closes the claim that explicit parent links add necessary information in this finite laboratory: inherited source/method labels suffice for the registered decisions. Closure 2 does not close provenance usefulness: F does not match G/L in all primary cells. It is a joint diagnostic/revision criterion, not proof specifically of improved culprit localization. Nevertheless the price forecast and the universal root forecast are partly negative. Less remaining error is not uniformly cheaper, and correct culprit localization is not the same as complete repair.

This is a one-fault, finite-memory diagnostic laboratory with reliable current catalogues, correct local operations, synthetic independent observations and an accessible common execution service. It does not test multiple simultaneous faults, wrong trusted definitions, arbitrary noisy observations, bounded labels under eviction, or optimal diagnosis. Singleton tests and explicit tariffs constrain the search result. G/L equality follows from equal accessible candidate sets and the registered set-based rule; no richer graph-specific planning was tested. L lacks parent pointers only in its policy memory: every policy shares the paid recursive execution service, whose recipes retain a graph. This is not graphless reconstruction. Traversal and set-processing runtime are not charged separately, so equal scientific-action tariffs do not establish equal computational efficiency. The regime condition changes an observed method catalogue record; it does not discover a wrong authoritative validity domain. Signal resolution checks the initiating observations or conflicting endpoints, not global correctness of all accepted claims. The findings concern scientific truth maintenance, not neural synchronization or phenomenal consciousness.
